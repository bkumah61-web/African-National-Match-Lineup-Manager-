How African Nations Match Queue Works

FIFO Data Structure in Tactical Management

National team coaches across Africa use a FIFO (First-In, First-Out) queue data structure on tactical tablets to manage starting line-ups, bench players, and live game substitutions.

1. Enqueue (`push`): When preparing tactical changes, bench players are queued to the rear (end) of the substitution list.
2. Priority Next (`unshift`): "Sub Next" places a player immediately at Index 0 for emergency substitution.
3. Dequeue (`shift`): When making the substitution, the player at the front (Index 0) replaces a Starting XI player on the field!
