FIFO Data Structure Documentation

Tactical Squad Management Architecture

About the Queue

A Queue is a linear data structure that follows the strict FIFO (First-In, First-Out) principle. In match operations, the first player placed into the substitution queue is the first one authorized to step onto the pitch when a tactical substitution is triggered.

Positions

Front (Index 0): The head of the queue representing the active element ready for immediate execution (the next player ready to sub onto the field).
Rear (End): The tail of the queue where newly queued substitutes are appended.

WHY QUEUE is USED

It guarantees strict chronological order during high-pressure games, prevents overlapping tactical instructions, and ensures fair and transparent squad rotation without chaotic intervention.

THE FLOW

Enqueue (`push`): Appends a bench player to the rear of the queue.
Priority Next (`unshift`): Places a player at Index 0 for emergency tactical changes.
Dequeue (`shift`): Removes the player at Index 0 when executing the substitution onto the pitch.
